About LifeTag

LifeTag is a digital health identity platform that stores critical medical information securely. In emergency situations, doctors can verify their identity and instantly access your records to provide immediate, informed care. Our mission is to make every second count when it matters most.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision: To revolutionize emergency healthcare with secure, instant access to medical data.

Mission: To empower individuals and healthcare professionals through safe, technology-driven solutions that save lives.
